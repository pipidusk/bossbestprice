Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

BossBestPrice is a price comparison app for Singapore. You take a photo of a product or type its name, and the app shows where it's sold and for how much. This policy explains what data that involves, who it goes to and why. In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and your scan history stays on your phone.

What we collect, and why

Our server keeps short technical logs (for example which product was searched, how long each store took, and errors) so we can fix problems and improve accuracy. Logs are kept for a limited time and aren't used to identify you.

What stays on your phone

Your history of scans and searches, including the photos and the prices found, is saved only on your phone. We never receive a copy. You can delete single items or clear everything in the History tab, and uninstalling the app removes it all. If you take a photo with the in-app camera, a copy is also saved to your photo album, as the app tells you when it asks for permission.

Services we use

To do its job, BossBestPrice sends the minimum needed to these services:

  • Anthropic (Claude) identifies the product in your photo and checks that listings match. It receives the photo and product details. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models.
  • SerpApi runs Google searches for us, including Google Lens image search. It receives the product name, and a smaller copy of your photo for image search.
  • Shopee, Lazada and other store programmes may receive product search terms when we look up their listings directly.
  • Render hosts our server in Singapore.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy. We don't send them your name, contact details or app ID.

Affiliate links

Some links in the app are affiliate links (for example Amazon.sg, Shopee and Lazada). If you buy something after tapping one, the store may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. The store sees that you came from BossBestPrice, and its own privacy policy applies once you're on its site or app. As an Amazon Associate, BossBestPrice earns from qualifying purchases.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't use your location.

Children

BossBestPrice is a general shopping tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't keep accounts or store your photos, there's usually nothing on our side tied to you. You can clear your history in the app at any time. If you have a question about your data, or want to make a request under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, email us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
