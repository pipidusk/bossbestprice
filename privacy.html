Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

BossBestPrice is a price comparison app for Singapore. You paste a product link, type a name, scan a barcode or take a photo, and the app shows where it's sold and for how much. It can also watch prices for you and tell you when there may be money to claim back through a store's price match. This policy explains what data that involves, who it goes to and why. In short: no accounts, we don't sell your data, and your lists and history stay on your phone. Invoices you choose to add to a purchase are kept on our server so you don't lose them. The only ads are optional videos you choose to watch.

What stays on your phone

Your shopping lists, your search history (including photos and the prices found) and a copy of your app ID are saved on your phone. Invoices or receipts you add to a purchase are saved on our server with that purchase (so they're still there if you move to a new phone), in a folder that only your app ID can open; removing the invoice or the purchase deletes it. We don't receive your lists or history, except that tapping "Update prices" on a list sends its items' shop links and prices to our server so we can re-check them. You can delete your history in the app, and uninstalling the app removes your lists and history. On iPhone, the app ID stays in the keychain so it's the same if you reinstall.

Ads

BossBestPrice has no banner ads. If you use the free plan, you can choose to watch a short video ad to get an extra search. These ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, Google may collect information such as device identifiers (for example your phone's advertising ID), your IP address and how you interact with the ad, and uses it under its own policy to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If we use personalised ads, your iPhone will first ask whether you allow the app to track you, and you can say no. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. If you don't want to see ads at all, simply don't watch them, or use Plus, which has no ads.

Services we use

To do its job, BossBestPrice sends the minimum needed to these services:

  • Anthropic (Claude) identifies the product in your photo or search, checks that listings match, and helps with answers like model lists and gift ideas. It receives the photo, product details and what you searched for. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models.
  • SerpApi runs Google searches for us, including Google Lens image search. It receives the product name, and a copy of your photo for image search.
  • Store affiliate programmes (Shopee, Lazada and Amazon Associates) are used to make store links. See "Affiliate links" below.
  • Google AdMob serves the optional rewarded video ads, as described above.
  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat handle Plus subscriptions. You pay through the App Store or Google Play, so we never see your card details. RevenueCat receives your app ID and your subscription status so the app knows you have Plus.
  • Expo's push notification service delivers price-drop and price-match alerts to your phone. It receives your push notification token and the alert.
  • Render hosts our server.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy. We don't send them your name or contact details, because we don't have them.

Affiliate links

Some links in the app are affiliate links (for example Amazon.sg, Shopee and Lazada). If you buy something after tapping one, the store may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. The store sees that you came from BossBestPrice, and its own privacy policy applies once you're on its site or app. As an Amazon Associate, BossBestPrice earns from qualifying purchases.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show banner ads. The only ads are the optional videos described above.
  • We don't use third-party analytics trackers. To know which features matter, our own server counts how often each one is used (for example "searches by photo" or "items added to a list"), and how many phones used it each day. These counts don't include what you searched or bought, and phones are counted with a code that changes every day, so they can't be traced back to you.
  • We don't ask for your location or your contacts.

Children

BossBestPrice is a general shopping tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices and rights

You can clear your history in the app, and delete monitored items and price-match purchases in the app, which removes them from our server. You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings. If you have a question about your data, or want to make a request under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, email us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
