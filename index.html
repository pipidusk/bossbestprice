FAQ

Which stores does it check?

Shopee, Lazada, Amazon.sg and Carousell, plus Taobao and Temu (clearly marked as shipping from China), Google Shopping, and the Singapore shops that usually stock that kind of item. Amazon.sg prices are only shown when they come from Amazon itself. Otherwise you'll see "See price on Amazon.sg".

Is it free? What's Plus?

Yes, it's free to use. Free gives you 3 searches a day, plus 1 more for each short video ad you choose to watch (up to 5 a day). Plus costs S$2.99 a month or S$24.99 a year and gives you 30 searches a day with no ads, more monitored items, purchases and lists, and top-ups if you need more searches.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new phone gets Plus free for 7 days, no card needed. If you go back to free, your lists stay on your phone, but only the first 3 you made can be opened.

Are there ads?

Only if you choose. There are no banner ads. Free users can watch a short video ad for an extra search. Plus has no ads at all.

How does price match work?

Many stores in Singapore offer price matching, each with its own terms and time limit. Tap "Bought it" after buying, and Boss keeps checking prices during the window. If it finds a lower price, it shows you the amount and the listing to show the store. Always check the store's own terms before you go.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, and the app never asks for your name, email or phone number.

What if I change phones?

On your old phone, open Saved, then Bought, and tap "Moving to a new phone?" to get a one-time code. Enter it on your new phone, and your monitored items, purchases, invoices and Plus come along.

What happens to my photos and invoices?

A photo you search with is sent to our server only to work out what the product is. It's held in memory for up to 15 minutes, then discarded. Invoices you add to a purchase are saved on our server so you don't lose them, and only your app can open them. Removing the invoice or the purchase deletes it. Your search history stays on your phone. Nothing is sold or used for advertising. The details are in our privacy policy.

Does this website track me?

No. This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Links in the app to some stores contain affiliate tracking codes, so the store knows the purchase came from BossBestPrice.
