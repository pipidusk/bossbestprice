Inside the app

Why BossBestPrice

Photo first

Most price checkers make you type a product name or scan a barcode. Just point your camera at it, even at unbranded or hard-to-name items.

The right shops for each item

Beyond the big marketplaces, it checks where people usually buy that kind of thing: Courts and Harvey Norman for furniture, Watsons and Guardian for personal care, and more.

Exact matches, honestly labelled

Accessories and knockoffs are filtered out. Second-hand deals are kept but clearly marked, and near-matches are shown separately.

Delivered price, not sticker price

Shipping is added in, so a cheap listing with expensive delivery doesn't fool you.

History that remembers

Every scan and search is saved with the prices found, so you can come back later and check again in one tap.

Built for Singapore

Prices in SGD from shops that sell here. Taobao and Temu deals are included but clearly marked as shipping from China.

FAQ

Which stores does it check?

Shopee, Lazada, Amazon.sg and Carousell on every search, plus Taobao and Temu, the Singapore shops that usually sell that kind of item, and Google Shopping.

Will it be free?

Yes. The app will be free to use.

When does it launch?

It's being tested now, with a launch on iOS and Android planned soon.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo is sent to our identification and image-search services only to work out what the product is and find it online. It isn't sold, and it isn't used for advertising. Your scan history, including photos, is saved only on your phone, and you can delete it anytime. The full details are in our privacy policy.

Does this website track me?

No. This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. Links in the app to some stores contain affiliate tracking codes, so the store knows the purchase came from BossBestPrice.
