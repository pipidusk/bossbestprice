Common questions

Which stores does it check?

Shopee, Lazada, Amazon.sg and Carousell, plus Taobao and Temu (clearly marked as shipping from China), Google Shopping, and the Singapore shops that usually stock that kind of item.

Is BossBestPrice free? What's Plus?

Yes, it's free to use. The free plan gives you 3 searches a day, plus 1 more search for each short video ad you choose to watch (up to 5 a day). You can monitor 3 items, track 1 price-match purchase and keep 3 lists.

Plus costs S$2.99 a month or S$24.99 a year. You get 30 searches a day with no ads, 20 monitored items, 10 price-match purchases and 50 lists. Some store links are affiliate links, so we may earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you.

Who's the little mascot?

That's Tag, our P.R.I.C.E.: Probably Really Innovative Consumer Expert. Everyone calls him Boss. He hunts down the best price for you, keeps an eye on the things you're watching and helps you stick to your shopping budgets.

What about my invoices?

You can add the invoice or receipt for anything you bought (a photo, or a PDF up to 5 MB) under Saved › Bought. It's saved with your purchase on our server, so it's still there if you move to a new phone (use "Moving to a new phone?" on the Plus page to get a transfer code). Photos are shrunk to save data.

How does the free trial work?

Every new phone gets Plus free for 7 days. No card is needed, and nothing is charged when it ends. After the trial, you're on the free plan unless you choose to subscribe.

How do I subscribe or cancel Plus?

Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, go to your Apple or Google Play subscription settings on your phone.

I went back to free and can't open some lists

Your lists stay on your phone, but on the free plan only the first 3 you made can be opened. Subscribe to Plus again to open the rest.

"You've reached today's limit"

Free users get 3 searches a day. You can watch a short video ad for 1 more search (up to 5 a day), or upgrade to Plus for 30 searches a day with no ads. Plus members who run out can top up with a one-off pack (10 searches for S$0.99, or 30 for S$2.49) that doesn't expire. The limit resets the next day.

Are there ads?

Only if you choose. There are no banner ads. On the free plan, you can watch a short video ad to get an extra search. Plus has no ads.

Why does it ask me to pick a model or size?

Some searches are broad, like "casio watch" or "kdk ceiling fan", so Boss asks you to pick a range or model first. Some products come in versions with very different prices, like a 13-inch or 15-inch MacBook Air M5, or iPhones with different storage, so it asks which one you mean. That way you compare the right thing.

It found the wrong product

Try another way to search: type the brand and model, paste a link to the product, or scan its barcode. For photos, a clear view of the label or logo works best. If a listing is clearly the wrong product, email us so we can improve the matching.

No prices were found

Some items, like rare collectibles, aren't sold in Singapore, or not online. Try searching with the brand and model, or a slightly broader name.

A price is different on the store's page

Prices come from store listings at the moment you search, and sellers change them often. Shipping also depends on the seller. Always check the listing before buying.

Why does Amazon.sg say "See price on Amazon.sg"?

We only show Amazon.sg prices when the item is sold by Amazon itself. For other sellers on Amazon.sg, tap through to see the price there.

What do the labels on results mean?

The cheapest delivered price is tagged. Second-hand deals are marked, so check the listing for its condition. Taobao and Temu listings are marked as shipping from China. "Several options" means the listing bundles several versions at different prices, so check you're picking the right one on the shop's page.

What is "Found it cheaper?"

If you find a listing cheaper than the ones shown, you can add it. The link and price you enter are shown to everyone searching that item. No personal details are stored with it.

How do price-drop alerts work?

Save an item to monitor it, and the app checks its price every day. You get a notification when the price drops, as long as you've allowed notifications for BossBestPrice.

How does price match work?

Many big stores in Singapore, such as electronics and furniture chains, offer price matching, each with its own terms and time limit. After buying, tap "Bought it". The app keeps checking prices for the price-match window and tells you if there's money to claim back. For stores whose terms it knows (currently Courts and Harvey Norman), it works out the exact amount. For other stores, it still tells you if the price dropped, so you can ask them. Each store decides whether to approve a claim.

Why can't the app add items to my cart?

Shopee and Lazada don't let apps fill your cart. Instead, tap "Buy" on a shopping list, and the app opens each item in turn so you can add it to your cart yourself.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo is sent to our server only to work out what the product is. It's held in memory for up to 15 minutes, then discarded, and never saved to disk. Your history, including photos, stays on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my history or monitored items?

You can clear your history and delete monitored items and price-match purchases in the app. Your history and lists are only stored on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.

"Can't reach the server"

Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email us: the service may be briefly down for maintenance.
