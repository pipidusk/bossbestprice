Common questions

It identified the wrong product

Tap "Not right?" under the product name and pick the right one, or type its brand and model. The app searches again straight away, and it learns from the correction for next time. Photos where the label or logo is visible work best.

No prices were found

Some items, like rare collectibles, aren't sold in Singapore, or not online. Try the Search tab with the brand and model, or use the Look elsewhere buttons to open Shopee, Lazada, Carousell or Google Shopping with the search ready.

A price is different on the store's page

Prices come from store listings at the moment you search, and sellers change them often. Shipping also depends on the seller. Always check the listing before buying. If a listing is clearly the wrong product, email us so we can improve the matching.

What do * and † mean?

* means second-hand: check the listing for its condition. † means it ships from China (Taobao or Temu). Delivery fees and times for those aren't included in the price shown.

"You've reached today's limit"

To keep BossBestPrice free, each phone can do 20 scans a day. The limit resets at midnight Singapore time.

"Can't reach the server"

Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email us: the service may be briefly down for maintenance.

How do I delete my history?

Go to the History tab and tap Clear all, or open a single item and tap Delete. Your history is only stored on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.

Is BossBestPrice free?

Yes. Some store links are affiliate links, so we may earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. That's how we keep the app free.
